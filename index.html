About School of Quran

Quran education made accessible for everyone.

School of Quran is an online Quran learning academy based in Sylhet, Bangladesh.


Our educational levels include Tajweed, Nazira and Hifzul Quran.


We aim to bring correct Quranic recitation and Quranic knowledge to people in an organized and accessible way.

How We Teach

A structured and student-focused Quran learning system.

🎯

Assessment

Every student is assessed according to their current Quran reading and learning level before joining the appropriate class.

📖

Tajweed & Correct Recitation

Students learn pronunciation, articulation points and important rules of Quranic recitation step by step.

🔁

Daily Practice & Revision

Regular practice and revision help students maintain accuracy and consistency in their learning.

🏆

Progress Monitoring

Students' progress is monitored regularly so that teachers can identify weaknesses and provide appropriate guidance.

Our Mission

Our goal is to deliver correct Quranic recitation and Quranic knowledge to people from all walks of life.


Many people cannot attend regular educational institutions because of lack of time or opportunity. We aim to bring structured, institutional-style Quran education to them through flexible online learning.
