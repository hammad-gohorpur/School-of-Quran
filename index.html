About School of Quran

Quran education made accessible for everyone.

School of Quran is an online Quran learning academy based in Sylhet, Bangladesh.


Our educational levels include Tajweed, Nazira and Hifzul Quran.


We aim to bring correct Quranic recitation and Quranic knowledge to people in an organized and accessible way.
